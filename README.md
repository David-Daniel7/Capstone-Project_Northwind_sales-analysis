# Capstone-Project_Northwind_sales-analysis
📌 Project Overview  This capstone project focuses on end-to-end business analytics using structured transactional data. The objective is to derive actionable insights across customers, orders, employees, products, suppliers, and shippers to support data-driven decision-making.  T
🛠️ Tools & Technologies
SQL (Exploratory Data Analysis)
Databricks (Serverless Data Warehouse, SQL Notebooks, Genie Assistant)
Power BI (Data Visualization & Dashboards)
GitHub (Version Control & Documentation)

📊 Analysis Modules
1. Customer Analysis

The customer analysis focuses on understanding purchasing behavior, identifying high-value customers, and uncovering trends in order frequency and product preferences.
Key Metrics & Insights:
Total revenue per customer
Average Order Value (AOV)
Order frequency
Customer segmentation based on:
Revenue contribution
Purchase patterns
Geographic location
Business Impact:
Identifies top-performing customers
Detects potential customer churn
Provides insights for targeted marketing, sales strategies, and operational improvements

2. Order Analysis

Order analysis evaluates overall sales performance and order trends.
Key Metrics & Insights:
Total orders and total revenue
Order trends over time
Peak sales periods
Top-selling products
Delivery and fulfillment efficiency
Business Impact:
Supports inventory optimization
Improves order fulfillment processes
Enhances customer satisfaction

3. Employee Analysis

Employee analysis examines workforce data to understand staffing structure and experience levels.
Key Metrics & Insights:
Total number of employees
Employee tenure
Hire dates
Job titles and roles
Employee location distribution
Business Impact:
Identifies workload and role distribution
Supports workforce planning
Aids performance evaluation and operational planning

4. Product Analysis

Product analysis evaluates inventory and sales performance across product categories.
Key Metrics & Insights:
Units in stock
Units on order
Unit price
Category-wise product distribution
Discontinued product status
Business Impact:
Identifies top-selling and underperforming products
Optimizes inventory levels
Supports pricing and marketing decisions

5. Supplier Analysis

Supplier analysis focuses on procurement and supplier performance.
Key Metrics & Insights:
Total number of suppliers
Products supplied by each supplier
Geographic distribution of suppliers
Business Impact:
Identifies reliable and high-performing suppliers
Improves procurement efficiency
Supports inventory planning and risk management

6. Shipper Analysis
Shipper analysis evaluates logistics and delivery performance.
Key Metrics & Insights:
Shipment volume
Shipping frequency
Delivery performance trends
Business Impact:
Optimizes logistics operations
Ensures timely deliveries
Improves overall customer satisfaction

📈 Key Outcomes

Comprehensive business performance insights
Clear identification of revenue drivers
Improved understanding of customer and operational behavior
Data-backed recommendations for business optimization

🚀 Conclusion

This capstone project demonstrates practical data analytics skills, combining SQL-based EDA, cloud data platforms, and business intelligence visualization. The insights generated can directly support strategic decision-making across sales, operations, HR, and supply chain functions.
